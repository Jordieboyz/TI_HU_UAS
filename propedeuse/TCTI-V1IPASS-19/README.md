# TI Individual Propaedeutic Assessmen

The Individual Propaedeutic Assessment (IPASS) is the direction-specific conclusion of the propaedeutic year. In this project, the student must demonstrate that they can independently apply what they have learned in the propaedeutic phase.

For the Technical Informatics direction, the student must demonstrate their ability to write technical software, including accompanying documentation, testing, and demonstration. They are given the freedom to choose their own topic within the technical framework of the project and to emphasize their own focus: "With a high degree of independence, using modern informatics techniques, the student addresses a technical problem and delivers a product that substantially contributes to the organization's short and long-term goals."
