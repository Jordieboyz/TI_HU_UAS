# Applied Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a hot topic with its potential applications still being discovered. Software giants like Google and Apple are increasingly embracing the possibilities of AI, but what exactly can you do with it?

The course delves into the possibilities of AI, specifically focusing on the use of Neural Networks (NN), Deep Learning (TensorFlow), and Genetic/Evolutionary Algorithms (EA).

NN is a technique that has recently experienced a resurgence due to the use of Deep Learning by companies like Google in its image software and GO-playing computer. Neural networks are inspired by the human brain and are capable of classifying large amounts of data or discovering patterns.
